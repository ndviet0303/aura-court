## Scope safeguards and pending operational evidence

1. Publication/payment features require verified receiver and provider sandbox evidence; flags alone never establish compliance.
2. Legacy balances/refunds/payables cannot be converted or written off by this proposal. The cutover gate requires a recorded disposition and reconciliation.
3. Preserve report separation of customer memberships and partner service plans, and separation of post-session informational bills from advance split-payment collection.
4. Empty report sections are tracked as documentation gaps, not new invented implementation requirements. Existing repository performance/recovery/privacy contracts remain in force.
5. New findings discovered during implementation must be linked to the corresponding report rule and reviewed for scope, rather than silently enlarging this change.
